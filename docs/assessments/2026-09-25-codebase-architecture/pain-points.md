# Pain-point register

Every verified pain point from the analysis, one row each, grouped by subsystem and sorted by severity. IDs are stable: the refactor plan (../../plans/codebase-refactor.md) cites them. Verdict records the adversarial verification: *confirmed*, *partially confirmed* (real, but the original evidence was overstated or mis-cited; the evidence column shows the corrected citation), or *added* (found by the verifier). Themes and context are in [assessment.md](assessment.md), §9.3.

Totals: 284 pain points — 58 high, 155 medium, 71 low.
